# Hi, I'm Filip 👋

Designer who builds small, free tools. Everything here is plain JavaScript you can read, with your data staying on your computer wherever possible.

- **[Subtitle Overlay](https://github.com/Cleedek/subtitle-overlay):** subtitles on any web video, translated into 31 languages, two languages at once, your own style.
- **[Snap Translate](https://github.com/Cleedek/snap-translate):** translates the text in pictures right where they are on the page.
- **[Edge Trim](https://github.com/Cleedek/edge-trim):** crops transparent pictures right to the edge of what's in them.

More at [cleedek.com](https://cleedek.com).
